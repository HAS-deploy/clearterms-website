TermPlainly vs LegalZoom: Lease and Contract Review

When you're staring at a 40-page commercial lease or a vendor agreement stuffed with legalese, you have two very different tools available: TermPlainly, an AI-powered document translator that converts dense contract language into plain English, and LegalZoom, a long-standing legal services platform that connects users with licensed attorneys and offers a range of document-related services. They solve related problems in fundamentally different ways, and the right choice depends heavily on what you actually need.

What Each Tool Actually Does

TermPlainly

TermPlainly is purpose-built for document comprehension. You drop in a PDF or DOCX file and receive a plain-English breakdown of the document in seconds. It doesn't draft documents, and it doesn't connect you with a lawyer. What it does — quickly and cheaply — is answer the question: what does this thing actually say?

LegalZoom

LegalZoom is a full-service legal platform. For contract and lease review, its primary offering is attorney-assisted review: a licensed attorney reads your document, flags issues, and may suggest revisions or negotiate terms on your behalf. LegalZoom also offers document templates, business formation services, and ongoing legal plan subscriptions. It's a much broader platform, with contract review being one piece of a larger ecosystem.

Head-to-Head: Key Comparison Areas

Speed

| Factor | TermPlainly | LegalZoom | |---|---|---| | Turnaround | Seconds | Hours to days | | Human involvement | None | Attorney review |

TermPlainly wins on speed — no question. If you receive a lease on a Friday afternoon and need to understand it before a weekend meeting, TermPlainly delivers. LegalZoom attorney review typically takes one to several business days depending on the service tier and document complexity.

Depth and Legal Authority

This is where LegalZoom earns its place. A licensed attorney reviewing your commercial lease can do things TermPlainly cannot:

  • Identify clauses that are legally unenforceable in your jurisdiction
  • Flag terms that are unusual for your local market (e.g., an aggressive personal guarantee in a commercial lease)
  • Recommend specific revisions and language changes
  • Advise you on negotiation strategy
  • Provide an opinion you can act on with legal backing

TermPlainly tells you what the contract says in simple language. It doesn't tell you whether what it says is fair, dangerous, or legally sound in your specific state or situation. That distinction matters.

Accuracy and Reliability

TermPlainly's AI translation is strong for comprehension — it reliably paraphrases standard contract language clearly. However, highly ambiguous provisions, jurisdiction-specific carve-outs, or unusual clause structures can occasionally be oversimplified. You're getting a summary, not a legal opinion.

LegalZoom's attorney review is only as good as the attorney assigned, but because it involves a human with a law license and professional accountability, it carries more authority and catches nuanced issues that AI tools can miss.

Pricing

| Service | Approximate Cost | |---|---| | TermPlainly | Low monthly or per-use fee | | LegalZoom attorney review | $150–$500+ per document |

TermPlainly is dramatically cheaper. LegalZoom's contract review pricing varies — simple residential lease reviews may run around $150–$200, while complex commercial contracts can push $500 or more. LegalZoom also offers a legal plan subscription (roughly $30–$50/month) that includes some attorney access, but document review often still involves additional fees or limited minutes of consultation.

TermPlainly is the clear winner on affordability, particularly for users who need to review multiple documents regularly.

Ease of Use

Both tools are accessible without a law degree. TermPlainly's interface is as simple as it gets: upload a file, get results. LegalZoom requires setting up an account, selecting the right service, uploading the document, and then waiting. For one-off users, LegalZoom's platform can feel bureaucratic. For ongoing subscribers, it becomes more familiar.

What Types of Documents Each Handles

TermPlainly handles any PDF or DOCX you throw at it — residential leases, commercial leases, freelance contracts, NDAs, service agreements, employment contracts. The AI doesn't specialize; it processes.

LegalZoom's attorney review is better calibrated for higher-stakes documents: commercial leases, business contracts, partnership agreements. Attorneys can apply contextual expertise that generic AI cannot. For a straightforward residential lease, LegalZoom may be more firepower than necessary.

Real Pros and Cons

TermPlainly

Pros

  • Instant results — seconds, not days
  • Affordable, especially for frequent document review
  • No need to schedule anything or wait for a callback
  • Works across any document type
  • Great for understanding what you're signing before asking follow-up questions

Cons

  • Provides comprehension, not legal counsel
  • Cannot tell you if a clause is unusual, unfair, or unenforceable
  • No jurisdiction-specific guidance
  • Not appropriate as your sole resource for high-stakes contracts
  • AI can occasionally oversimplify nuanced or ambiguous provisions

LegalZoom

Pros

  • Licensed attorney reviews your document
  • Jurisdiction-aware advice
  • Can recommend edits and flag problematic clauses with legal authority
  • Useful for complex commercial documents where professional guidance matters
  • Access to broader legal services through subscription

Cons

  • Significantly more expensive per document
  • Turnaround is hours to days, not instant
  • Attorney quality can vary
  • May be overkill for simple, standard documents
  • Platform navigation can feel clunky for occasional users

When TermPlainly Is the Right Call

Use TermPlainly when your primary goal is understanding the document:

  • You're reviewing a residential lease and want to know what you're agreeing to before signing
  • You're a freelancer receiving a standard client contract and want to check for red flags before engaging a lawyer
  • You're reviewing multiple NDAs or vendor agreements regularly and need speed and volume
  • You've already spoken with an attorney and want a quick reference summary of the final version
  • Budget is tight and the document is relatively low-stakes

TermPlainly is particularly well-suited to people who are document-literate but not legally trained — you know the questions to ask, you just need the dense language decoded first.

When LegalZoom Is the Right Call

Use LegalZoom's attorney review when professional judgment is what you actually need:

  • You're signing a commercial lease for a business location with significant financial commitments
  • The contract involves unusual terms, large sums of money, or complex liability clauses
  • You want legally defensible advice, not just comprehension
  • You're entering a multi-year agreement where negotiation could save you thousands
  • You need someone to tell you if a clause is standard in your market or a red flag

LegalZoom is worth the cost when the downside of misunderstanding the contract exceeds what you'd pay for professional review.

A Practical Hybrid Approach

These tools don't have to be mutually exclusive. Many users benefit from using TermPlainly first to get a plain-English overview of the document, identify the sections they're uncertain about, and arrive at any attorney consultation with focused questions — whether that consultation is through LegalZoom or elsewhere. This approach saves time on the attorney's end, which can translate to lower billable hours or a more efficient LegalZoom review session.

TermPlainly handles the translation. LegalZoom handles the judgment call. Used together, they're more powerful than either alone.

The Bottom Line

Pick TermPlainly if you need to understand a document quickly, affordably, and without waiting — and you're comfortable doing your own due diligence or the stakes are manageable.

Pick LegalZoom if you need a licensed attorney to review a high-value or complex contract, offer jurisdiction-specific guidance, and give you advice you can act on with professional backing.

TermPlainly is a sharper, faster scalpel for comprehension. LegalZoom is a broader tool with more authority when the situation demands it. Neither is universally better — they're built for different moments in the contract review process.
